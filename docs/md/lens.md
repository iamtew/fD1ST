# Lens

Click a lens node on the Path. One route shows a single Lens; Stack, Split, Prism, Axis, and Cavity add more.

- **On / Mute / Solo** — whether this lens is in the sum.
- **Style** — 34 shapers. Drive and makeup follow the style table.
- **Filter** — pre-shaper filter type and Hz / Q.
- **Drive, Bias, Mix, Level** — how hard it hits, where the curve sits, how much of it you keep.
- **Bass / Mid / Treble / Presence** — tilt after the shaper.
- **Dyn, Pan, Ring** — dynamics, stereo place, and the per-lens delay loop.

The Transfer Focus is the I/O plot. Sage is dry. Emerald is wet. Click another node to leave.

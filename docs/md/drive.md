# Drive

Launch Focus is Drive until you click a Path node. In and Drive share this workbench.

- **Drive** — input gain into the first lens.
- **Tone** — tilt before the shaper.
- **Color** — color compensation on the output of the chain.
- **Channel** — stereo vs mid/side handling at the front.

The Transfer curve still draws lens 0 in One. That is the shaper, even while Drive is focused.
